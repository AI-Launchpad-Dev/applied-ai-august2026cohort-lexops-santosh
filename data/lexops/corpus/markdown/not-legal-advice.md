# Output Disclaimer and Use Restrictions Standard

# NORTHWIND SYSTEMS INTERNAL LEGAL DEPARTMENT
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CONTRACT-ANALYSIS OUTPUT GOVERNANCE
**Document Reference:** SOP-LEG-2024-04  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024  
**Applies To:** All Legal Department Personnel (Attorneys, Paralegals, Contract Managers)  
**Approved By:** Marcus Vance, General Counsel  

---

### 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure governs the internal generation, review, modification, and external distribution of any work product produced by Northwind Systems’ proprietary or licensed contract-analysis tools, including the "Northwind ClauseMatrix" and "Aegis AI Reviewer" modules (collectively, the "System").

1.2 Compliance with this SOP is mandatory for all members of the three-person legal team (General Counsel, Senior Corporate Counsel, and Legal Operations Manager) and any internal business units utilizing System outputs under legal supervision.

---

### 2. STATUS OF OUTPUT: DRAFTING ASSISTANCE ONLY

2.1 All contract-analysis, redlining, summarization, and risk-scoring generated by the System constitutes **solely automated drafting assistance**. 

2.2 System output does not constitute legal advice, formal legal opinion, or binding contractual interpretation. 

2.3 No automated output shall be relied upon by any Northwind Systems employee as a definitive legal determination regarding the legality, risk profile, or binding nature of any contractual provision.

---

### 3. MANDATORY ATTORNEY REVIEW THRESHOLD

3.1 **Zero-Tolerance External Distribution Rule:** Under no circumstances may any raw, unedited, or attorney-unreviewed output generated by the System be transmitted, emailed, displayed, or otherwise communicated to any external counterparty, customer, vendor, partner, or opposing counsel.

3.2 **Named-Attorney Sign-Off:** Before any contract clause, redline, summary, or negotiation position derived from the System is sent to an external party, it must undergo substantive review and explicit written sign-off by one of the following authorized named attorneys:
*   Marcus Vance (General Counsel)
*   Elena Rostova (Senior Corporate Counsel)

3.3 **Review Turnaround SLA:** Authorized named attorneys shall complete or reassign the review of queued System outputs within 4 business hours of generation during standard operating hours (09:00 to 17:00 EST, Monday through Friday).

---

### 4. MANDATORY DISCLAIMER Wording

4.1 Every internal summary, risk report, or briefing document generated via the System that references contract analysis must prominently display the exact statutory disclaimer set forth in Section 4.2 in 10-point bold font at the top of the first page and immediately preceding any concluding recommendation.

4.2 **Required Text:**
> **"NOTICE: THIS DOCUMENT CONTAINS AUTOMATED CONTRACT-ANALYSIS OUTPUT GENERATED BY NORTHWIND SYSTEMS SYSTEMS. THIS CONTENT IS PROVIDED FOR INTERNAL DRAFTING ASSISTANCE ONLY AND DOES NOT CONSTITUTE LEGAL ADVICE. IT HAS NOT BEEN FULLY VETTED FOR ALL JURISDICTIONAL NUANCES. RELIANCE UPON THIS OUTPUT WITHOUT INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION AND WRITTEN APPROVAL FROM NAMED COUNSEL (MARCUS VANCE OR ELENA ROSTOVA) IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED."**

---

### 5. PROHIBITED TERMINOLOGY: ENFORCEABILITY RULE

5.1 The System has been hard-coded to restrict vocabulary, and human users are strictly prohibited from generating, approving, or endorsing any output containing specific prohibited legal assertions.

5.2 **Absolute Prohibition:** The System and its human operators must **never** state, imply, categorize, or conclude that any specific contract clause is "enforceable" or "unenforceable."

5.3 **Permitted Alternative Phrasing:** Instead of prohibited enforceability determinations, legal personnel must use conditional, risk-weighted framing approved by the General Counsel, such as:
*   *Permitted:* "Presents substantial litigation risk under current Delaware case law."
*   *Permitted:* "May be subject to heightened judicial scrutiny regarding limitation of liability caps."
*   *Prohibited:* "This liability cap is unenforceable."
*   *Prohibited:* "This indemnity clause is fully enforceable."

---

### 6. AUDIT AND NON-COMPLIANCE

6.1 The Legal Operations Manager shall conduct weekly randomized audits of at least 15 outbound contract transmissions to verify compliance with Section 3 and Section 4.

6.2 Any breach of this SOP—specifically including unauthorized external transmission of unreviewed AI output or unauthorized declarations of clause enforceability—shall be reported directly to the General Counsel within 24 hours for internal remediation and logging under Northwind Systems Compliance Policy CP-902.
